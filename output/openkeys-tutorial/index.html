02 / SOUND

A mixer that thinks like a keyboard player.

Every channel is a playable layer. Split the keyboard, transpose, shape velocity and sustain, then build a musical balance with effects that belong to the sound.

  • 01Mix eight full SoundFont tracks, with four optional Lite Tracks
  • 02Set key ranges, transpose, velocity, sustain, and pitch
  • 03Shape ADSR, filters, EQ, reverb, chorus, delay, and OpenSky
