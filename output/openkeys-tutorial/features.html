01Base app

Your live mixer

Eight full instrument tracks. A balance you can see and play.

Features & requirements
  • Layer SF2 instruments with independent volume, pan, mute, solo, and arm controls.
  • Select, load, clear, and reorder tracks; watch track and master output meters.
  • Protect your setup with performance lock, Panic, and optional panic/mute on patch change.
  • Undo completed edits with the button or a two-finger left swipe; up to 30 interactions.
  • Keep playing with the background playback service and interruption/recovery handling.

Phone and tablet layouts adapt the mixer and performance controls to available space.

02Experimental

Four extra Lite Tracks

Experimental 12-track mode for smaller instruments.

Features & requirements
  • Keep the eight standard tracks and add basic SF2 playback on tracks 9–12.
  • 32 voices per Lite Track; 16 MB or smaller SF2s recommended.
  • 32 MB maximum per Lite SF2, with 64 MB combined across Lite Tracks.
  • Lite Tracks retain basic mix, pitch, MIDI routing, and supported control assignments.

Lite Tracks do not include effects, filters, advanced envelope processing, arpeggiator, or sequencer. Extra tracks increase CPU and RAM use.

03Base app

Setlists & patches

Prepare the show, then move through it confidently.

Features & requirements
  • Create, rename, duplicate, delete, and select setlists; import or export a setlist.
  • Create, save, rename, duplicate, delete, reorder, and import/export patches.
  • Move to the previous or next patch, or copy a patch into another setlist.
  • Choose Save & Copy or copy the last saved version when a patch has unsaved changes.
  • New patches start with empty tracks and default track effects; duplicates retain the source setup.
  • Save master level, Play In/Hear In key, learned controls, drum setup, and linked song with a patch.
  • Protected Default entries, missing-sound notices, session recovery, and backup persistence help retain your work.

Patch/setlist files reference sounds. Use Pro Sound Packs when you need to include permitted SF2 files.

04Base app

Scenes & per-scene splits

One song, up to eight performance moments.

Features & requirements
  • Create, update, rename, duplicate, reorder, and recall scenes, with a protected Default scene.
  • Use a scene strip or larger grid, choose an accent color, and set transition time.
  • Save modulation-wheel position with its own transition duration.
  • Toggle per-scene key ranges for each patch, or keep one shared split across scenes.
  • Recall scenes with on-screen buttons, MIDI Note/CC assignments, or PC numpad 1–8.
  • Link a scene to an OpenNotes song part; OpenNotes requires Pro.

Scenes recall supported mix/effect states. Loaded SF2 files and presets stay with the patch; there is no per-scene sound-file switch.

05Base app

OpenLiveboard & keyboard response

Play on the screen or shape the feel of your controller.

Features & requirements
  • Multitouch on-screen keyboard with touch-position velocity, pressed notes, and layer/range overlays.
  • Dedicated 76-note phone keyboard view and tablet keyboard integration.
  • Transpose, octave shift, fine tune, fixed velocity, input velocity range, and nine Dynamic Touch levels.
  • Choose a MIDI device and Omni or individual MIDI channel for each track.
  • Set keyboard zones, sustain latch, and options to ignore volume, sustain, pitch bend, modulation, or MIDI CC.
06Base app

Pitch, expression & movement

Give each layer its own response.

Features & requirements
  • Pitch-bend range and center dead zone, with an on-screen pitch wheel.
  • Normal modulation, low-pass sweep, or high-pass sweep, with depth control.
  • Expression pedal CC11 with minimum/maximum and Linear, Soft, or Hard curves.
  • Channel/polyphonic aftertouch enable and depth.
  • Glide/portamento with adjustable time and Always or Legato-only behavior.

Advanced processing is available on the eight standard tracks; Lite Track restrictions apply.

07Base app

USB, Bluetooth & MIDI Learn

Make your keyboard, pedals, pads, and faders part of the rig.

Features & requirements
  • Android USB MIDI and BLE MIDI discovery/connection, including permission guidance.
  • Use All Keyboards or select one globally; filter devices and channels per track.
  • Stable saved controller identities and patch-scoped assignments preserve control mappings.
  • Choose Keys, Mixer, or Pads + Scenes roles and Normal, Mackie, or Auto MIDI protocol.
  • Learn supported CC, Note/Button, Program Change, and Pitch Bend controls; review, save/cancel, or clear assignments.
  • Map mixer/effect controls, master level, OpenPads volume, scenes, and supported Pro workspace actions.
  • Optional direct USB MIDI for supported Arturia, Yamaha, M-Audio, Behringer, Roland, Korg, and M-VAVE/SINCO devices.

Controller compatibility depends on the device and its MIDI mode. Direct USB MIDI is separate from direct USB audio.

08Base app

Mackie controller support

Hands-on mix control with a defined set of supported actions.

Features & requirements
  • Track and master faders; pan encoders; mute, solo, arm, and track select.
  • Stop invokes Panic; Rewind/Fast Forward move between scenes.

Mackie Play, Record, and bank/channel navigation buttons are not active in the current version.

09Base app

Tone, envelopes & EQ

Build the character of a sound before adding atmosphere.

Features & requirements
  • Gain, ADSR envelope, filter cutoff/resonance, and fine tuning.
  • Piano Character: Natural, Warm, Bright, Vintage, Stage, and Intimate.
  • Track 1 eight-band EQ; three-band EQ on the other standard tracks, with presets.
  • Advanced EQ: high-pass, low/high shelves, four parametric bands, and low-pass.
  • Advanced EQ band enable, frequency, gain, Q, output trim, and presets.
  • Reverb, chorus, and per-track delay with mix and sound-shaping controls.

Advanced EQ takes priority while enabled; the original EQ settings remain saved for bypass.

10Pro

OpenSky, tremolo & shared ambience

Bring space and motion into the performance.

Features & requirements
  • Tremolo with depth, free-running frequency, tempo sync, subdivision, and stereo options.
  • OpenSky: Impulse, Cloud, Shimmer, Bloom, Chorale, Magneto, Nonlinear, Room, Hall, Chamber, Plate, Spring.
  • Type-specific OpenSky controls including decay, pre-delay, tone, modulation, and mix.
  • FX Return enable and wet level without muting the dry instrument.
  • Shared sends/returns A–D with enable, mute, solo, return level, pan, and width.
  • OpenSky X styles: Aerial, Swell, Vapor, Horizon, Vault, Drift, Deep, Glass.
  • OpenSky X size, decay, diffusion, pre-delay, tone, low end/cut, modulation, shimmer, and regeneration.

Shared FX currently provides shared ambience/return processing. Distinct shared Stereo Delay and Custom processor modes are not fully available; per-track delay is separate.

11Pro

Arpeggiator & instrument sequencer

Turn held notes into evolving patterns.

Features & requirements
  • 15 arp patterns: Up, Down, Up/Down variants, As Played, Reverse Played, Random, Chord, Converge, Diverge, and doubled patterns.
  • Chord voicings from major/minor and suspended shapes to sevenths, ninths, power fifths, and octaves.
  • Octave span, latch, gate, and quarter/eighth/triplet/sixteenth/thirty-second rates.
  • Patch tempo from 40–240 BPM and shared rhythmic subdivision.
  • Looping instrument sequencer with up to 16 steps, individual notes, fallback note, and adjustable length.
  • MIDI/keyboard note entry, pattern clearing, velocity, rate, and gate controls.

The instrument sequencer follows track/input activation. It is not a full song-arrangement timeline.

12Base app

SF2 library & sound preview

Find, audition, and organize your instruments.

Features & requirements
  • Import SF2 files, select a bank/program preset, and assign it to a track.
  • Browse folders or 13 instrument categories; view file sizes and organize sounds by name.

RAM, storage, and Lite Track limits still apply with Pro. Managed content has its own deletion/protection rules.

16Base app

OpenPads SoundFont

Sustained atmosphere that follows the song.

Features & requirements
  • Twelve root keys and an eligible SoundFont/preset library with the built-in OpenKeys Pad.
  • Major, Minor, Sus2, Sus4, Major7, Minor7, fifth, Add9, and Octave harmonies; open voicing.

21Pro

MIDI & MP3 Player

Rehearse with a backing track or play MIDI through your sounds.

Features & requirements
  • MIDI Type 0/1 import and playback through the current patch, with supported controller/expression messages.
  • Play, Pause, Stop, seek, loop, volume, and MIDI speed from 50–200%.
  • MIDI metadata/progress and section-marker recall of matching scenes.
  • MP3/audio backing-file import, streamed playback, looping, seeking, and volume.

OpenRecord stereo studio

Record the output. Review the take. Share the performance.

Features & requirements
  • One stereo master track capturing eligible OpenKeys output, including app media playback.
  • 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV recording streamed to disk.
  • Live L/R waveform overview, meters, elapsed time, and take selection.
  • Review with Play/Pause/Stop, return-to-start, waveform seeking, timeline scrolling, and up to 16× zoom.
  • Library actions: play, rename, delete, save/export, and share; recover eligible interrupted WAV files.

Requires Android 10+ and capture consent; unavailable in Ultra. Records app output, not microphone input. One stereo track; no multitrack overdub, waveform editing, or capture pause/resume.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

A few things to know.

Can I record while using Ultra?

No. Select another audio mode for OpenRecord. Output recording requires Android 10 or later; app-only video requires Android 14 or later.

Does a scene load a different SF2?

Scenes store supported mix/effect states and optional key ranges. SF2 file/preset selection remains a patch/track setting.

Does twelve-track mode give me twelve full processing channels?

No. Tracks 9–12 are experimental Lite Tracks for small SF2 files, with 32 voices each and no advanced processing. The eight standard tracks retain their full feature set.

Do the drum kits and Japzki Bells need another download?

No additional on-demand download is needed for these included files. Aneira, TheRedPiano, Darchrow, and Zero are optional downloads with the access requirements shown above.

Is OpenRecord a multitrack editor?

It records one stereo master track per take, with waveform review, seek, zoom, and export. It does not provide multitrack overdubbing, waveform editing, or microphone recording.

What kind of USB audio and tutorial support is available?

OpenStream uses available Android/system audio routes; direct USB audio is disabled in this version. The offline visual guide is available now. A native button-by-button walkthrough is not included.
