01Base app + experimental

Performance workspace and mixer

16 listed capabilities · v1.51

Full feature list & requirements
  • Eight standard SF2 instrument tracks/layers.
  • Experimental expansion to twelve tracks; tracks 9–12 are Lite Tracks.
  • Select a track, load or clear its instrument, and display its assigned sound.
  • Layer multiple sounds and define independent keyboard splits/zones.
  • Per-track volume, pan, mute, solo, and arm controls.
  • Master output volume and master/track level metering, including stereo peak data.
  • Reorder tracks by dragging; standard and Lite Tracks remain in their respective groups.
  • Horizontally scrolling mixer tracks where space requires it.
  • Track editor tabs: Sound, Key Range, MIDI, Performance, and Effects.
  • Visible edited/loading states and patch-loading progress.
  • Performance lock to protect editing controls while playing.
  • Undo button and two-finger left-swipe undo gesture; history is bounded to 30 completed interactions.
  • Panic/all-sounds-off control.
  • Optional panic/mute behavior during patch changes.
  • Background playback service and audio interruption/recovery handling. Background performance defaults on; its separate settings toggle is currently hidden.
  • Exit confirmation and foreground/background lifecycle management.

Lite limits: basic SF2 playback; 32 voices per Lite Track; ≤16 MiB recommended per SF2, 32 MiB maximum per SF2, 64 MiB combined. FX, filters, ADSR processing, arpeggiator, sequencer, and advanced processing are disabled on Lite Tracks. Only the basic MIDI Learn target subset is retained. This is experimental capacity, not twelve identical full-feature tracks.

03Base app + Pro tools

Scenes

11 listed capabilities · v1.51

Full feature list & requirements
  • Up to eight scenes per patch, including its Default scene.
  • Scene strip, enlarged scene grid, and scene editor.
  • Create, save/update, rename, duplicate, move, delete, and recall scenes, with Default protections.
  • Scene name up to 24 characters and an individual accent color.
  • Set a scene transition duration, including immediate changes.
  • Save modulation-wheel position and a separate modulation transition duration.
  • Smooth scene modulation interpolation and visible transition progress.
  • MIDI Note or CC binding for scene recall.
  • PC numpad 1–8 recalls scenes 1–8 when enabled.
  • Scene-to-OpenNotes song/section cues.
  • Keyboard split ranges can be stored separately per scene when the patch's switch is enabled.

Exact scene scope: snapshots store track volume, arm/mute/solo, ADSR enabled state, reverb/chorus/delay settings and selections, EQ, tremolo, FX-return state/level, and optional key bounds; scene-level metadata stores modulation, transition settings, color, MIDI binding, and OpenNotes cue. A scene is not a full copy of every track property. Loaded SF2/preset identity and full envelope parameters remain patch/track properties; there is no implemented per-scene SF2-change toggle.

06Base app + Pro tools

MIDI connectivity and controller assignment

16 listed capabilities · v1.51

Full feature list & requirements
  • Android USB MIDI input, including multiple connected devices.
  • BLE MIDI discovery and connection with Bluetooth permission guidance and retry handling.
  • Global All Keyboards selection or a chosen keyboard; independent per-track device/channel filters.
  • Stable device identities for stored controller assignments across reconnects.
  • Global live keyboard selection is kept separate from a recalled patch's learned controls.
  • Per-device Normal MIDI, Mackie MIDI, or Auto protocol selection.
  • Per-device role: Keys, Mixer, or Pads + Scenes.
  • Optional direct USB MIDI input for supported Arturia, Yamaha, M-Audio, Behringer, Roland, Korg, and M-VAVE/SINCO identities. This is separate from the disabled direct USB audio transport.
  • MIDI Learn supports CC, Note/Button, Program Change, and Pitch Bend assignments where the target allows them.
  • Long-press learn entry on supported controls, detected-message review, explicit Save/Cancel, and clearing mappings.
  • Learnable master volume and OpenPads volume.
  • Learnable track mixer, sustain/transpose/glide, ADSR/filter/tuning, EQ, tremolo, OpenSky, FX sends, reverb, chorus, and delay controls.
  • MIDI scene recall, OpenNotes navigation/scrolling shortcuts, and OpenDrums pad triggering.
  • Axiom-family button/latch and program-button-prefix handling.
  • Mackie faders, master fader, pan encoders, mute, solo, arm, and track select.
  • Mackie Stop invokes panic; Rewind/Fast Forward move between scenes.

Mackie limit: Play, Record, Bank Left/Right, and Channel Left/Right decode but are ignored by the current controller. Do not describe this as complete Mackie transport/banking support. The audited routing is app instrument control; it does not establish general external MIDI-output sequencing.

The newer native button-by-button walkthrough with Next/Exit, runtime highlighting, start-from-control/chapter, and progress/resume is still a documentation plan. The current guide's chapter list does not give OpenDrums, Player, or OpenRecord their own chapters, so it is not comprehensive documentation of the latest feature set.

    BEFORE YOU PLAY

    A few things to know.

    Can I record while using Ultra?

    No. Select another audio mode for OpenRecord. Output recording requires Android 10 or later; app-only video requires Android 14 or later.

    Does a scene load a different SF2?

    Scenes store supported mix/effect states and optional key ranges. SF2 file/preset selection remains a patch/track setting.

    Does twelve-track mode give me twelve full processing channels?

    No. Tracks 9–12 are experimental Lite Tracks for small SF2 files, with 32 voices each and no advanced processing. The eight standard tracks retain their full feature set.

    Do the drum kits and Japzki Bells need another download?

    No additional on-demand download is needed for these included files. Aneira, TheRedPiano, Darchrow, and Zero are optional downloads with the access requirements shown above.

    Is OpenRecord a multitrack editor?

    It records one stereo master track per take, with waveform review, seek, zoom, and export. It does not provide multitrack overdubbing, waveform editing, or microphone recording.

    What kind of USB audio and tutorial support is available?

    OpenStream uses available Android/system audio routes; direct USB audio is disabled in this version. The offline visual guide is available now. A native button-by-button walkthrough is not included.
