OpenKeys Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short:
  • OpenKeys Pro does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • There are no ads, analytics, or tracking in the app.
  • Your patches, setlists, scenes, and recordings stay on your device.

Who we are

OpenKeys Pro ("the app") is published by OpenLabs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone and iPad.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the app. We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or device identifiers. We don't use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the app stays in the app's storage on your device:

  • patches, setlists, scenes, and mixer settings
  • SoundFonts you import or download
  • MIDI recordings

We can't see this data. It is removed when you delete the app. It may be included in your device backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Downloading sounds

When you download a Sound Bundle or SoundFont, the app requests the file from our download server (downloads.openkeys.pro). As with any web request, the server receives your IP address and the name of the file requested. That information is used only to deliver the file and keep the service running securely. We do not use it to identify you or build a profile, and we do not sell or share it.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as OpenKeys Pro, Darchrow Bundle, and Zero Bundle, are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation from Apple of which purchases your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them. Restoring purchases works the same way. Apple's handling of payment information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Bluetooth and MIDI

If you allow Bluetooth access, the app uses it only to find and connect to wireless MIDI keyboards and controllers. MIDI data from your devices is used only to play sound in the app, and it is never sent off your device.

Links to other sites

The app may link to our website and to our pages on social networks such as YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok. These sites are run by their own operators and have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

You can turn off Bluetooth access at any time in your device's Settings. You can remove all app data by deleting the app. Because we don't hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. If you have a question about this, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us through openkeys.pro.
