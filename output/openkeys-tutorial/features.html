Features

Tools for building sounds, preparing your set, and performing live.

Live Mixer

Layer instruments and balance volume, pan, mute, solo, and master output.

Setlists & Patches

Organize songs, save your sounds, and move through a performance.

Scenes & Key Splits

Recall song sections with smooth transitions and optional per-scene keyboard ranges.

OpenLiveboard

Play from the on-screen keyboard with touch response and visible layers.

Expression Controls

Shape velocity, sustain, pitch bend, modulation, aftertouch, and glide.

USB & Bluetooth MIDI

Connect keyboards, pedals, pads, and controllers with customizable MIDI assignments.

Sound Shaping & EQ

Adjust envelopes, filters, tuning, reverb, chorus, delay, and equalization.

OpenSky & Shared FX

Add atmospheric effects and shared ambience to your layers with Pro.

Arpeggiator & Step Sequencer

Build repeating note patterns and melodies with Pro.

SF2 Library

Import, preview, organize, and select SoundFonts and their presets.

Sound Collections

Explore included instruments and optional downloadable sound collections.

Portable Sound Packs

Move patches together with their permitted sounds using Pro.

OpenPads

Create sustained SoundFont pads with harmony, swell, and crossfades.

OpenPads Audio & Loops

Play key-aware audio loops; Pro adds ready-made audio pads and advanced processing.

OpenDrums

Play sixteen drum pads with included kits and custom samples using Pro.

Drum Backing & Sequencer

Choose backing styles or record and overdub your own drum sequences with Pro.

MIDI & MP3 Player

Play backing tracks, loop sections, and adjust playback with Pro.

OpenRecord

Capture stereo app audio or screen video, then review and share your takes.

OpenNotes

Organize lyrics, chords, song sections, and performance notes with Pro.

Live Lyrics & OCR

Follow scrolling lyrics and turn photos or PDFs into editable notes with Pro.

Audio Output & Monitoring

Choose your audio route and monitor output, CPU, RAM, and performance health.

Themes & Appearance

Personalize themes, fader colors, glow, trails, and phone or tablet controls.
